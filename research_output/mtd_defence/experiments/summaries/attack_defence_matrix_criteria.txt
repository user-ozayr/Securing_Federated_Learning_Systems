Verdicts (per study/attack/condition, means over seeds): 'no attack effect' if the attack lowers FedAvg baseline accuracy by < 0.02 and (backdoor) baseline ASR < 0.10; otherwise 'mitigated' if the condition's accuracy is within 0.02 of its own no-attack accuracy and (backdoor) its ASR < 0.10; 'partially mitigated' if it recovers >= 50 % of the baseline's accuracy loss, or (backdoor) halves the baseline ASR without losing more accuracy than the undefended baseline under the same attack; else 'not mitigated'. A destroyed model also has a low ASR, so a low ASR alone never counts; rows with last-3 accuracy < 0.2 are marked model_destroyed and their ASR is not meaningful. References come from the same study; a study without a run of its own uses the study that shares its setting (e1x, e2, e4 -> e1, then e1x; reference_defence -> smpc_defence, the same algorithm executed in SPDZ2k; '<attack>__default' -> '<attack>'), always over the same seeds. The *_reference columns name the runs used.
